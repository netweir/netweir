# Governance

## Today

James Conway ([@jamesconwaydev](https://github.com/jamesconwaydev)) started
Netweir, holds its copyright and makes the final call on decisions while the
project is small. Decisions happen in public, in issues, Discussions and
pull requests.

## Licensing

Netweir is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3,
and is also offered under a commercial licence to companies that can't use
AGPL code. That's why contributors agree to the [CLA](CLA.md) once, before
their first pull request is merged. You keep the copyright in your work; the
CLA gives the maintainer the licence needed to offer it both ways.

## How changes get in

Every change, including the maintainer's own, goes through a pull request
that passes CI on Linux, macOS and Windows. Nothing is pushed straight to
`main`. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) lists the checks and what a pull
request needs.

## Maintainers

Anyone who contributes steadily and reviews other people's work can be
invited to become a maintainer. Once there are at least two maintainers,
pull requests need an approving review from a maintainer other than the
author.
