# Code of Conduct

Netweir is built in the open by people who mostly have never met. This page
is what we owe each other while we do it. It covers the issue tracker, pull
requests, Discussions, and anywhere else you're speaking for or about the
project.

## What we expect

- Treat people with respect, including when you think they're wrong.
  Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Someone asking a basic question is new, not lazy.
- Give feedback you'd be glad to receive, and take it the same way.
- Credit other people's work, and own your mistakes when you make them.
- Welcome people whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender
  identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race,
  religion, or sexual orientation.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, in public or in private, including continuing to contact
  someone who has asked you to stop.
- Insults, slurs, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or an
  email, without their permission.
- Trolling, and deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Using Netweir's issue tracker or Discussions to plan scraping that
  breaks the law, or to share data taken from people who didn't agree to
  it.

## Reporting

If something here happens to you, or you see it happen, email
**conduct@netweir.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept
confidential, and answered within a week. Netweir has one maintainer for
now, so if your report is about the maintainer, you can take it to
[GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
instead.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened, the response may be a private word, a public
note on the thread, edited or removed comments, a temporary ban from the
project's spaces, or a permanent one. Whoever is reported gets a chance to
give their side before anything beyond a warning, unless waiting would put
someone at risk.
